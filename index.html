Els Issue Uploader

Internal single-user tool for the YouTube channel "엘's 이슈 (Els Issue)"

What it is

Els Issue Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the "엘's 이슈" YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads short videos produced by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel, with scheduled publish times (YouTube Data API v3).
  • Reads view and retention statistics of the owner's own channel to improve future content (YouTube Analytics API).

Videos are produced with AI-assisted tools (script drafting and voice) and are reviewed and approved by the channel owner before they are published. AI disclosure is applied where required by YouTube policy.
